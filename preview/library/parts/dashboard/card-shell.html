⚠ The padding difference between the two cards is measured, not an error. stat-card is 12, metric-card is 16.

⚠ metric-card does not fit card-layout's analytics slot. That slot's measured grid height is 196 — two rows of the v1 analytics-card 94 floor plus the 8 gap. metric-card's content (a body-12-med label, a Dashboard/display-28-med value and a sub-line) bottoms out around 104 at p-12, so two rows come to ~214 and the stretched lead stat-card is dragged ~16px past its own 198 ceiling. Measured 17 Aug 2026.

The 196 was measured with analytics-card, whose value is smaller. There is no combination of v2 cards that reproduces it. Either the slot's height is no longer 196 in a v2 build, or the analytics slot needs a card that is not metric-card. Unresolved — flag it in the build rather than shrinking the type to force the old number.
