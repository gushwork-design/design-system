Profile pictures

One mark, built from a 3×3 grid of dots. The colour says what level someone is; the pattern is theirs, picked from a fixed set by their email. No Google avatars, and nothing fetched from anywhere.

The corner arc comes out at about 7.4 design px, which lands on --gw-radius-8. Every other number above is a node property. If the tile is meant to be rounder than that, it is a one-line change.

2

Twelve patterns

Generated under a rule rather than drawn by hand, so the set can grow the same way later. Every pattern fills the centre cell, carries four to six dots, touches all three rows and all three columns, and is either symmetric or orthogonally connected. Rotations and mirrors of the same shape count as one. Fourteen shapes satisfy that; these are twelve of them, with the two from the Figma first.

3

Every variation

Three levels × twelve patterns — 36 marks. Colour is the level and is never random; the pattern is the person.

How a person gets theirs

A small hash of the signed-in email picks the index, so the same person keeps the same mark on every device and across sessions — random-looking, never actually random. Colour comes from the account level, so two people at the same level can share a pattern but a pattern never implies a level on its own.
