FAQs

Questions
before you book

Something else on your mind?
Talk to us on the demo call.

Is it really live in under 24 hours?

Yes. After your demo call we build your pipeline, move your records, and switch the AI agents on within 24 hours.

What do free setup and migration include?

We set up your stages, fields, and follow-up rules, then move your contacts, deals, and history from spreadsheets, inboxes, or your current CRM. There is no setup fee.

How does automatic follow-up work?

You set the rules, like when a quote should get a check-in. The follow-up agent sends it on time, every time, and re-engages leads that went quiet.

What else do the AI agents do?

They flag and chase renewals, send reminders on overdue invoices, and keep your pipeline up to date, so your team spends its time on the deals that need a person.

Do I have to replace the tools I already use?

Tell us what you use today on the demo call. We’ll show you what moves into Gushwork and what can stay where it is.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your team and how you sell, so we quote it on the demo call. Setup and migration are free either way.
