Gushwork Style Guide

Last updated 07th Sep, 2026

Everything you need to design on-brand for Gushwork lives here — the palette, the type scale, the spacing and radius rules, and the components they compose into. When a decision is already made, this guide is where it’s written down, so you can spend your time building instead of guessing.

Concept

We’re enablers of lean exponential growth. Our logo features an exponential curve, a direct reminder & leading indicator of this message. The upward curve transitioning from thick to thin represents our core mission, maximizing ARR per FTE.

We free businesses from bloated teams at a time where even one-person unicorns can thrive and Gushwork is the gush behind that transformation. Through our on-demand marketing services, we remove subjectivity and productize workflows with humans in the loop, ensuring both quality and quantity. That is faster growth with leaner teams.

This logo serves as a constant reminder of our promise: hockey stick growth and limitless potential for businesses that partner with us.

Logo

The mark is the exponential curve. Keep it consistent — same shape, same proportions — everywhere it appears: web, decks, dashboards, print. Give it clear space, use one of the three approved treatments below, and never redraw or restyle it.

Incorrect usage

The mark carries the brand, so protect it. Don’t recolor it, tilt it, stretch it, or add effects the system doesn’t use. If you’re reaching for a gradient or a shadow, stop.

Correct usage

Pick the treatment by background, not by preference. These cover every surface you’ll build.

Typography

Typography establishes visual hierarchy, guides the reader’s eye, and sets the tone for the entire design. A well-defined type system ensures consistency across screens, improves readability, and reinforces brand identity at every touchpoint.

Spacing & Radius

A consistent spacing scale brings rhythm and structure to layouts, ensuring elements breathe and align predictably across every component.

Consistent corner rounding creates visual harmony across components, softening edges to convey approachability and modern aesthetics. Every step is a multiple of 4 except 2px and 10px.

Elevation

Shadows separate a surface from the one behind it. The ramp is short on purpose — s1 through s4 for stacking, plus two special cases.

Visual elements

The grid is the brand’s visual element, and it runs in four colourways — Flat Black, Primary, Neutral/25 and near-white. The construction holds everywhere: square cells, a one-pixel line, a few cells filled to break the regularity. The pitch does not: it is set per surface, so the page hero runs 40px cells in Primary/600 while the OG card runs roughly 111px cells whose line carries a radial grey-to-black fade — which is why that one ships as an exported vector rather than CSS. The filled cells carry the meaning: step them diagonally for movement, or stack them into rising columns for growth.

The shape studies pair a plain rounded slab with one whose bottom-right corner sweeps open — that curve is our logo, abstracted. The shape works as a placeholder that holds an image, as a graphic element on its own, or as the block a layout is built from.
