Drops.Family of agents
from Gushwork.

The concept

Inspired by water droplets, Drops brings Gushwork’s family of AI agents to life. Each agent has its own role and personality, while sharing a common visual identity that connects it to the larger family.

The character

A soft, rounded silhouette, closely paired ivory eyes and a gentle smile make Drop feel friendly and approachable. Its powder-blue satin silicone finish adds a tactile quality, while short arms and small feet keep the form simple and recognizable.

Outfits & accessories

Each agent’s role comes to life through its equipment. SEO Drop carries a magnifying glass and leather briefcase, Boost/Paid Ad Drop takes flight with a twin rocket pack, and Email Marketing Drop holds campaign envelopes. These props distinguish each specialist while preserving the same familiar character.

The character

Their naturally varied shapes and sizes become characters through eyes, mouths and personality. Drops are Gushwork’s family of AI agents—each working in a different area for you, with its own strengths and a shared identity.
