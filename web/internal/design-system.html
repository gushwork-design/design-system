Design System

Last updated 3rd Oct, 2026

The Design System is where Gushwork’s design decisions are kept: the foundations, the components and the templates that everything we make is built from. It is managed here too. New and changed items wait in Review until the owner approves, reworks or rejects them, and only what is approved reaches the Library and the skills.

Library shows everything approved for building with Gushwork: the foundations (colour, type, spacing, radius), the components, and the templates to start from.
