Templates

These are not downloads. Each one lives in the design system itself, so installing the plugin is how you get them — and how you keep getting them when they change. Copy a template to a new name and fill it in; tokens, fonts and brand assets resolve out of the repo, so no template owns its own copy of anything.

Case study page One customer story, told as a page. Hero with the outcome numbers, a prose column with a sticky rail CTA, and the closing CTA fold. Ships with an og-source.html for the social card. Nothing to build — open it in a browser. skills/gushwork-web/templates/case-study/copies a prompt
